- Añade una previsualización estable al redimensionar con la proporción bloqueada.

# KeystoneClient 0.15.9

## Fixes

- Adds a stable resize preview when the aspect ratio is locked.
  - Shows a translucent outline for the target size while the real window remains still during the drag.
  - Hides the preview and applies the real size once when the pointer is released.
